## Worldbuilding: The Favor Exchange

An illustrative seed says **“The future owes me a favor.”** This fixture is assistant-authored from the supplied conversation context, not a new human choice or a verbatim archival quote.

The **Borrowed Tomorrow workshop** offers three branches:

- Music: a narrator spends confidence now and hears the bill arrive in the hook.
- Arcade: borrow one extra action; the next turn loses that action. A debt may change play, but never charge money.
- Town: a fictional Favor Desk tracks voluntary story commitments; participants can decline, revise, or leave. No real obligation follows from fictional applause.

Residents include the Ledgerkeeper, who remembers consequences, and the Renegade Genius, who challenges whether a rule still earns its upkeep. These are invented roles, not accounts, admitted agents, or people with authority.

A participant can keep a fragment untouched, fork an interpretation, contest attribution, or choose an alternate consequence. Collaboration preserves each contribution and audience. A possible “Quirk Fucked” grade asks whether the unexpected consequence is memorable, emotionally precise, playable/useful, and worth its maintenance. That grade requires actual human judgment; no automated score grants it.
