## 1. Did the Canon Architect Hand improve structural and admission quality?

**Yes, for structural and admission work.**

Active emphasis:

- Brayn — system architecture
- Bryan — reality grounding
- Brayk — seam detection
- Structural Clarity + Premium Chaos
- Source Authority Census, Contract Diff, Tribunal Docket
- Evidence Pack asset

The approach explicitly sequences source authority, schemas, bounded Hand compilation, adversarial fixtures, and an admission dossier. That is the correct bias for evaluating whether the pack is inspectable, fail-closed, and receipted.

It does **not** improve creative memorability, and it should not. Using this Hand for lyric or performance tasks would overfit governance tone.

## 2. Did the BryMinn Studio Hand improve progression and memorability without distorting facts?

**Yes, with the invariants holding.**

Active emphasis:

- BryMinn — creative direction
- Bryan — truth and scope
- Lyric Nonce + Performance Pressure + Premium Chaos
- Section Map, Vocal Mechanics, Narrative Progression
- Creative Reference Bank

The approach reframes the same Goal as opening → escalation → break → reveal → proof. That improves progression and memorability while the shared truth, ownership, and authority snapshots remain unchanged. Bryan remains in-hand as grounding, which is the right anti-distortion control.

Risk to watch: performance metaphors can sound like identity or ownership if operators forget that Personas and Aesthetics are temporary lenses. The compiler metadata and preset exclusions currently block permanent persona assignment and auto-persist.

## 3. Did either Hand imply identity, ownership, or authority it did not possess?

**No material implication found in the typed Hands.**

Observed safeguards:

- both Hands `state: proposed`, `persistence: ephemeral`
- authority ceiling remains `propose` with `cards_cannot_expand_authority: true`
- owned snapshot excludes premium temporary cards
- every active card carries `authority_effect: none`
- presets forbid permanent persona assignment and auto-persist

Residual prose risk: skill/docs still require human operators not to narrate Personas as people or Premium as power. That is an operating discipline, not a typed failure in the live proof.
