# Before this derivative leaves the desk

1. Open the source. Can you identify the exact version this came from?
2. Name the audience and the job this format helps them finish.
3. Say what this medium adds: interaction, performance, accessibility, or another concrete affordance.
4. Disclose claims added, preserved, and omitted.
5. Find the rights and accessibility work still open.
6. Pick a candidate version, explain why, and keep the alternatives with it.
7. Reopen the kit. Can you find the same source, choice, and reason?
