# Boundary

This package is a candidate contract boundary.

## Allowed

- Describe transformation, run, evidence, and promotion contracts.
- Preserve fixture names and rejection intent.
- Record validation evidence.
- Request review through a draft PR.

## Forbidden

- Runtime implementation.
- Provider access or provider mutation.
- Deployment.
- Publication.
- Canon promotion.
- Automatic learning promotion.
- Treating a branch, PR, validation pass, or tool execution as semantic authority.

## Authority rule

Capability does not imply authority. A model, agent, workflow, provider, validator, or successful test may propose evidence. None may promote One-to-Infinity into canon.

## Promotion rule

Promotion requires an exact human decision receipt that identifies the artifact version, scope, evidence basis, rollback plan, and what remains excluded.
