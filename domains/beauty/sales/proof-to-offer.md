# Proof-to-Offer Ladder

The pack does not yet authorize a product claim. Commercialization advances only as evidence accumulates.

## Gate 1 — Constitutional proof

**Evidence:** one complete verified chain.  
**Allowed claim:** the system can preserve lineage and human authority across one Taste Engine cycle.  
**Forbidden claim:** recommendations improve outcomes.

## Gate 2 — Repeated personal proof

**Evidence:** the same participant completes multiple cycles and the next recommendation is compared against a baseline.  
**Allowed claim:** the system adapted in this participant's declared purpose.  
**Forbidden claim:** general accuracy.

## Gate 3 — Design-partner proof

**Evidence:** five or more participants complete repeated cycles; failure and correction rates are reported.  
**Candidate offer:** a guided personal Taste Trial or creator-audience preference experience.  
**Pricing:** open; test willingness to pay rather than inventing it.

## Gate 4 — Commercial proof

**Evidence:** paid use, retention, recommendation lift, and regret/return outcomes compared with a baseline.  
**Candidate offer:** consumer membership, creator tool, or licensed recommendation surface.  
**Constraint:** commercial relationships must be visible and cannot silently alter ranking.

## Sales evidence packet

A future seller should receive:

- verified proof receipt;
- participant narrative with consent;
- baseline comparison;
- correction and failure examples;
- what the system refuses;
- commercial relationship policy;
- security and data-boundary summary;
- precise claim language.

No sexy deck outranks missing proof. Typography is not an evidence primitive.
