# Taste Engine Proof Pilot — Candidate

## Honest promise

One participant will see whether a small sequence of explicit beauty choices can produce an understandable recommendation and a graph update they deliberately approve, revise, or reject.

## Not promised

- recommendation lift;
- sales conversion;
- universal shade or product matching;
- medical or skin analysis;
- autonomous personalization;
- a finished Quirk Beauty platform.

## Pilot unit

- one consenting participant;
- one declared purpose partition;
- one pairwise choice;
- at least two fresh candidate options;
- one testable recommendation;
- one explicit real-world outcome;
- one Human Gate decision;
- one core receipt.

## Kill conditions

Stop the pilot if the participant cannot understand why the recommendation was made, cannot abstain, cannot correct the evidence, cannot reject the graph change, or if any provider treats engagement as satisfaction.

## Offer ladder

1. **Proof only:** one observed run; no commercial claim.
2. **Repeatability:** five independent participants with complete receipts.
3. **Usefulness:** participants report that the process improved a real decision.
4. **Commercial experiment:** only after usefulness evidence; disclose every commercial relationship.
