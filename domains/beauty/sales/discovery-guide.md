# Candidate Discovery Guide

Use only after the first proof succeeds.

## Questions

1. What beauty decision do you repeat often enough for better memory to matter?
2. Where do current quizzes, creators, or stores misunderstand your taste?
3. What context changes your answer—budget, occasion, effort, season, lighting, or something else?
4. What would make a recommendation feel explainable rather than invasive?
5. Which outcomes can you honestly observe after using the recommendation?
6. What should the system never infer from your behavior?
7. Which preference changes would you want to approve, correct, or forget?
8. What would make this useful enough to repeat or pay for?

## Disqualifying answers

Do not force a fit when the person:

- wants diagnosis or treatment;
- cannot test an outcome;
- wants automated purchasing rather than decision support;
- does not care what the system remembers;
- expects guaranteed aesthetic truth;
- cannot consent to the data captured.
