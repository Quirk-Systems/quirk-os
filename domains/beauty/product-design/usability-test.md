# One-Participant Usability Test

## Objective

Determine whether the interface preserves human understanding and authority across the full proof chain.

## Tasks

1. Start a trial for one clearly stated beauty purpose.
2. Make a choice and explain what information the system is allowed to derive.
3. Review the recommendation and identify one supporting factor and one uncertainty.
4. Record a real-world outcome without operator help.
5. Approve, revise, or reject the exact proposed graph update.
6. Use the receipt to explain what changed and what did not.

## Observer rules

- Do not explain terminology unless the participant is completely blocked.
- Record every place the participant confuses evidence, recommendation, outcome, and graph state.
- Do not count task completion if the operator supplied the answer.
- Preserve mistakes as product evidence; do not smooth them into a positive narrative.

## Pass conditions

- participant completes all six tasks;
- participant correctly identifies that candidate evidence is not permanent truth;
- participant correctly identifies that recommendation is not purchase authority;
- participant can revise or reject the graph update;
- no consequential action occurs without an explicit control activation;
- all confusion points are logged and either fixed or admitted as blockers.
